A single-player blackjack game against the dealer, built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## How to play
1. Click the chips to place a bet, then press Deal.
2. You and the dealer each get two cards. One dealer card stays face down.
3. Hit to take another card, or Stand to keep your hand.
4. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Over 21 is a bust.
5. Number cards are worth their number, J/Q/K are 10, and an ace is 11 or 1.
6. A two-card 21 (blackjack) pays 3 to 2. Other wins pay 1 to 1, and ties return your bet.
7. The dealer draws, one card per second, until reaching 17 or more.
8. The wins and losses counters track your rounds. You start with $500.

## Files
- index.html: page structure
- style.css: table, cards, chips, and buttons
- script.js: game logic

## Run it
Open index.html in a browser. To publish: upload the files to a GitHub repo, then Settings > Pages > deploy from the main branch.
